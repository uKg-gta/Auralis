Audius catalogue

Auralis currently uses Audius read-only catalogue endpoints for discovery, search and streaming. Track availability and rights are controlled by creators and may change. Auralis does not claim ownership of catalogue music or artwork and does not bundle or download Audius audio.

Audius API documentation · Audius terms · Creator streaming controls

Audio metadata

Auralis reads metadata embedded in imported audio files, including common ID3, Vorbis, FLAC, WAV and MP4/M4A fields. Metadata and artwork remain subject to the rights and terms that apply to the original file and its creator.

Not currently integrated

YouTube / YouTube Music, Jamendo and MusicBrainz are not active catalogue integrations in this version. Their names are not an attribution or claim of partnership.
