Privacy Policy

Auralis is a static, local-first music player and uses YouTube API Services for public video search and playback. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent directly to music providers when you use their catalogs or players. You must agree to this policy before enabling YouTube features; see the Auralis Terms of Use.

Data stored on this device

Playlists, liked online tracks, listening history, preferences, and optional public API credentials are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. Auralis does not offer local audio importing or store personal audio files. YouTube and Jamendo library entries save provider IDs, links and your own library organization; provider metadata is held temporarily in page memory. Auralis keeps a last-checked time for saved YouTube references, refreshes them when YouTube is enabled, and removes references that have not been refreshed within 30 days. MusicBrainz metadata is used to enrich artist and recording information in the app. Use Export backup before clearing browser site data. You can remove stored information by deleting Auralis site data in your browser.

Requests to music services

  • Audius receives searches and requests for catalog data and streams. Audio is streamed from Audius and is not downloaded into Auralis storage.
  • Google and YouTube receive YouTube search requests only after you enable YouTube in Settings. Loading the official privacy-enhanced embedded player also sends data to YouTube; it may use cookies and collect data under the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube videos play only while the visible player is on screen; Auralis does not store their audio or video.
  • Jamendo receives searches for its Creative Commons catalog. Track audio streams from Jamendo and is not downloaded into Auralis storage.
  • MusicBrainz receives optional artist and recording lookup requests. Metadata lookup results are kept in memory for the current page session.

YouTube consent and terms

Before using YouTube search or playback, enable YouTube in Settings and agree to this policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. You can turn YouTube off in Settings. Search terms, video IDs and the embedded player are sent directly to YouTube or Google; Auralis does not send local audio or library contents.

Search terms, video IDs, and provider requests are sent directly from your browser to the relevant provider. The providers’ privacy policies and terms govern their own handling of those requests.

Offline use

Auralis is online-first. The service worker caches only the app shell; it does not cache provider API responses, Audius or Jamendo audio, or YouTube media. Music playback requires an internet connection.

Contact

Auralis is a non-commercial prototype. For privacy questions, contact the repository maintainer through the project’s public GitHub repository.
