Privacy

Your music stays yours.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Auralis is designed as a local-first music player. It does not require an account. Your imported audio and library information are stored in this browser on this device.

Information stored on your device

Auralis stores imported audio files, artwork, track metadata, playlists, tags, liked songs, lyrics, listening history, discovery preferences, queue state and profile details in browser storage (including IndexedDB and local storage). This information is used to provide the player, library, recommendations and settings. Auralis does not send this local library data to an Auralis-operated account service.

Online catalogue and streaming

When you browse, search or play catalogue tracks, Auralis communicates with Audius services. Requests may include the search text or track identifiers needed to find and stream the selected music. Audius receives those requests and may process connection data under its own policies and terms. Streaming requires an internet connection. Auralis does not download Audius audio into your library.

Read the Audius privacy policy and Audius terms for details about Audius services.

Analytics, advertising and accounts

The current Auralis app does not provide user accounts or an Auralis-operated analytics or advertising profile. Your browser, hosting provider, network provider and third-party catalogue services may process technical connection information as part of delivering their services; Auralis does not control those services.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Use Auralis settings to export a library backup before changing browsers or clearing site data.
  • Use the app's available library and history controls to remove information you no longer want stored.
  • Clearing Auralis site data in your browser can permanently remove imported audio and other local library data. Export a backup first.

Children

Auralis is a general-purpose music player and does not knowingly collect personal information through an account service. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online catalogue services.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when Auralis adds features or changes how data is handled. The date above indicates the latest revision.
