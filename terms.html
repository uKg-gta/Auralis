Music and rights

Auralis does not support importing or storing personal audio files. Catalog and streamed content remains subject to the rights and terms set by its provider and rights holders. Availability may vary by location, account, or embedding permission.

YouTube features

YouTube videos are played only through YouTube’s official, visible embedded player with its standard controls and branding. Auralis does not download, extract, cache, or play YouTube audio in the background. Playback may be unavailable for some videos; use the link to open those videos on YouTube.

Local storage and privacy

Auralis stores your playlists, likes, listening history and preferences in this browser. You can disable YouTube in Settings or delete Auralis site data through your browser. Read the Privacy Policy for details about provider requests and browser storage.
